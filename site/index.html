ENGLISH RELEASE · SYMBOLIC MATH

A symbolic math lab in your calculator.

KhiCAS adds exact algebra, calculus, equation solving, matrices, geometry, and graphing to the Casio fx-CG50. The 2026a line follows Fadouse's official development branch and keeps the engine focused on real calculator workloads.
