## Gaussian-erf family

For rational a>0, q>0, b!=0 and real rational d, and N=(m+1)/q a positive integer,

```
I = integral_0^infinity c*x^m*exp(-a*x^q+d)*erf(b*x^(q/2)) dx.
```

The monotone substitution t=x^(q/2) gives
`I=(2*c*exp(d)/q)*J_(N-1)`, where
`J_n=integral_0^infinity t^(2*n+1)*exp(-a*t^2)*erf(b*t) dt`.
Integration by parts yields

```
J_0 = b/(2*a*sqrt(a+b^2)),
J_n = (n/a)*J_(n-1)
    + b*(2*n-1)!!/(2^(n+1)*a*(a+b^2)^(n+1/2)).
```

Let v=a+b^2 and write `J_n=b*P_n/(2*a*sqrt(v))`.
Only rational scalars need recur:

```
M_0=1, P_0=1,
M_n=M_(n-1)*(2*n-1)/(2*v),
P_n=n*P_(n-1)/a+M_n.
I=c*exp(d)*b*P_(N-1)/(q*a*sqrt(v)).
```

Thus there is one final square root, no differentiation or erfc expansion.
The integrand near zero is O(x^(q*(N+1/2)-1)); at infinity a>0 supplies exponential decay. Negative b is handled directly by erf oddness. Bounds are exactly zero to positive infinity. q<=16 and N<=8 bound recurrence work. Powers in the shared sparse parser have numerator magnitude<=64 and denominator<=8; at most 64 visits are shared across the three parses. This is a conservative supported domain, not a claim that other N cannot converge.

## Cauchy angle family

For rational A>0, a!=0, q>0, positive integer n and denominator order k,

```
I = integral_0^infinity c*x^(q-1)*atan(a*x^q)^n
                      / [A*(1+a^2*x^(2*q))]^k dx.
```

With u=|a|*x^q and t=atan(u),

```
I = c*sign(a)^n/(q*|a|*A^k)
    * integral_0^(pi/2) t^n*cos(t)^(2*k-2) dt.
```

The measured powers are matched, not assumed: the denominator parser must prove constant term A>0, other exponent 2*q, and its coefficient A*a^2; the outside monomial exponent must equal q-1. These conditions imply a strictly positive denominator and ordinary convergence. At zero the order is x^(q*(n+1)-1); at infinity it is at most x^(-q*(2*k-1)-1).

For h=k-1, use the finite identity

```
cos(t)^(2*h)= [C(2*h,h)+2*sum_(j=1)^h C(2*h,h-j)*cos(2*j*t)]/4^h.
```

At L=pi/2 and omega=2*j, write `C_n=int_0^L t^n*cos(omega*t) dt`.
Two elementary integrations by parts give

```
C_0=0,
C_1=((-1)^j-1)/omega^2,
C_n=n*L^(n-1)*(-1)^j/omega^2 - n*(n-1)*C_(n-2)/omega^2.
```

The candidate uses n<=8 and k<=4, so there are at most three harmonic terms and seven recurrence steps per term. It handles rational scales and negative a; only integer angle powers are flattened, including signed scalar factors introduced by atan oddness. Fractional angle wrappers are rejected. The rule requires radian mode and exactly the positive half-line.
