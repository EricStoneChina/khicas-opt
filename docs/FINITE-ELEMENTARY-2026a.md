# Bounded finite elementary integration

The implementation handles rational multiples of sin, cos, exp, ln, atan, and abs of a real affine argument with nonzero rational slope, and rational powers of rational affine arguments, plus `1/(a*x^2+b)` for positive rational a,b. Endpoint constants are limited to rational values, rational affine expressions in pi, or exp of a rational constant, at most 32 syntax nodes. Integrands have at most 96 nodes. At most eight power wrappers are examined, each and the combined exponent have numerator magnitude at most 64 and denominator at most 16. No general integration, symbolic primitive tree, or limit calculation is invoked.

All direct formulas follow the substitution u=a*x+b and elementary antiderivatives. The atan primitive is u*atan(u)-log(1+u^2)/2; the absolute-value primitive is u*abs(u)/2. The positive quadratic formula follows u=sqrt(a/b)*x and has a strictly positive denominator everywhere. Sine, cosine, atan and the quadratic kernel require radian mode.

For ln, both transformed endpoints must be nonnegative; a nonconstant affine argument is then strictly positive in the interior. At an endpoint zero, u*ln(u) tends to zero, so the logarithmic singularity is integrable. Any fractional power wrapper requires a nonnegative affine base throughout the interval. For exponent p<=-1, both transformed endpoints must have the same strict sign, excluding both internal poles and divergent endpoint singularities; fractional wrappers restrict that sign to positive. For p>-1, zero endpoints are allowed. Integer powers can cross zero when continuous. The reciprocal primitive uses ln(abs(upper/lower))/a, which is valid on a pole-free negative interval too. Evaluated sqrt(x^2) may become abs(x); it is handled by the separate absolute-value rule, never by incorrectly identifying sqrt(x^2) with x across negative values. Unknown signs, nonrational parameters, unsupported bounds, nonlinear arguments, and budget excess reject to existing fallback.

The prior baseline dff6974 also crashed for x^2 on a 64 KiB guarded host thread, in the generic definite limit/parser path and glibc lazy symbol resolution. A diagnostic-only LD_BIND_NOW=1 removed that host failure; this is not evidence that the original generic path necessarily crashes on SH4. Production tests keep the ordinary environment. These shortcuts reduce generic call depth and avoid the internal parser/limit work without changing loader settings. Host timings are indicative only and must not be described as calculator speed ratios.

Direct tests: tests/finite_elementary_integrals.cc and tests/run-finite-elementary.py; 29 exact inputs in raw and evaluated form, plus 21 domain/resource rejection checks, run in normal and 64 KiB threads. Independent formula checks: `tests/verify-finite-elementary.py` and its saved JSON report, eleven 80-digit quadratures including shifted/signed affine functions, zero log endpoints, negative reciprocal interval and fractional powers. For x^(-2/3) the independent quadrature uses x=t^3 to remove the integrable endpoint singularity.
